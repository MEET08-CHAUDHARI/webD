Biryani

Biryani is my favorite food. It's a delicious rice dish with spices and meat.

I love the aroma and taste of biryani, especially when it's served with raita.

It's a popular dish in India and is often enjoyed during special occasions.

There are many variations of biryani, such as Hyderabadi, Lucknowi, and Kolkata biryani.

  1. Hyderabadi Biryani
  2. Lucknowi Biryani
  3. Kolkata Biryani
  4. Malabar Biryani
  5. Ambur Biryani
